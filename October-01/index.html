Meet the Panelists

Two Leaders Who've Made the Shift

Panelist 01

Dr. Gerald Kaplan

Prosthodontist · Dental Communication Coach · Founder, ProDentalCoach

Dr. Gerald Kaplan is an experienced dentist, practice mentor and founder of ProDental Coach. Drawing on more than five decades in dentistry, he helps dentists navigate the realities of practice that clinical education doesn't always prepare them for — leadership, communication, team culture, patient relationships and decision-making. Rather than prescribing generic solutions, he helps dental professionals step back, gain clarity and identify realistic next steps suited to their practices and values, with a particular interest in how emerging technology like AI can enhance dentistry without diminishing the human connection at the heart of excellent care.

Panelist 02

Angela Davis-Sullivan

Founder & CEO · Adaptive Dental Solutions

Angela Davis-Sullivan is a powerhouse in dental practice growth, blending 30+ years of hands-on experience with bold, results-driven coaching. From chairside to C-suite, she's done it all — and now helps dental teams and leaders rise to their full potential. A dynamic speaker, published author, and trusted voice in top industry publications, Angela transforms practices from the inside out. Her mission? Build strong systems, grow confident leaders, and turn chaos into clarity with heart, humor, and grit.

What We'll Cover

Five Leadership Shifts

01

The "founder trap" — why what built your practice now caps it

The habits and hustle that carried you through year five are the same ones quietly capping growth — and most owners don't notice until burnout forces the issue.

→
02

Your first real #2 hire: who, when, and what to hand over first

→
03

From best clinician in the room to best leader in the building

→
04
